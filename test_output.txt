PNEUMONIA CLINICAL GUIDELINES PRIMARY SYMPTOMS: - Fever above 38°C (100.4°F) Productive cough with sputum - Chest pain during breathing - Shortness of breath - Fatigue
and weakness DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSIS: - Community-acquired pneumonia (most
common) - Bronchitis - COVID-19 - Tuberculosis (if chronic) - Lung cancer (in elderly
smokers) RECOMMENDED TESTS: - Chest X-ray (confirmatory) - Complete Blood Count
(CBC) - C-Reactive Protein (CRP) - Sputum culture - Oxygen saturation monitoring
URGENCY ASSESSMENT: - High: Temperature >39°C, oxygen saturation <92%, respiratory
distress - Medium: Fever 38-39°C, productive cough, no breathing difficulty - Low: Mild
symptoms, normal oxygen levels TREATMENT RECOMMENDATIONS: - First-line
antibiotics: Amoxicillin 500mg TID for 7 days - Alternative: Azithromycin if penicillin allergy Supportive care: Rest, hydration, fever management - Follow-up chest X-ray in 6 weeks
ADDITIONAL NOTES: Elderly patients and immunocompromised individuals require closer
monitoring. Hospital admission recommended if respiratory rate >30/min or confusion
present. EOF
